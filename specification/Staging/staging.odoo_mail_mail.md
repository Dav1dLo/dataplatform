# odoo_mail_mail

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo, an open-source ERP and CRM platform. The naming convention (`mail_mail`, `mail_message_id`, `fetchmail_server_id`) and the presence of Odoo-specific audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, `write_date`) are characteristic of the Odoo `mail.mail` model, which manages the outgoing email queue.

## Functional process 
This table supports the communication and notification module within Odoo. It tracks the lifecycle of outgoing emails, including their scheduling, delivery state, and any associated failure reasons, facilitating the "Customer Communication" and "Automated Notification" business processes.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single outgoing email record in the Odoo mail queue. It acts as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `mail.mail` model, capturing the content, recipient metadata, and delivery status of messages processed by the system.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** `body_html`, `email_to`, and `email_cc` may contain PII or sensitive customer communication; ensure appropriate masking in downstream reporting.
- **Timezones:** Timestamps are stored in UTC as per Odoo standard practice.
- **Data Retention:** The `auto_delete` column suggests that some records may be purged from the source system shortly after processing; downstream incremental loads must account for potential missing historical data.
- **State Logic:** The `state` column is highly volatile and reflects the current status of the Odoo mail queue; it is not a historical log of status changes.
