# odoo_product_pricelist_item

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, as evidenced by the naming convention (`odoo_product_pricelist_item`), the presence of standard Odoo audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, `write_date`), and the specific functional mapping to Odoo's pricing engine architecture.

## Functional process 
This table supports the Sales and Pricing management process. It defines the specific rules, discounts, and fixed prices applied to products or product categories within various price lists, enabling dynamic pricing strategies based on quantity, time-bound promotions, and margin constraints.

## Description
One row represents a single pricing rule or condition associated with a specific Odoo pricelist. It defines how a price is calculated (e.g., fixed price, discount, or markup) for a product, category, or global scope. This is a raw landed copy of the Odoo `product.pricelist.item` model, serving as the staging entity for downstream pricing analytics.

- **Precision:** `NUMERIC` fields do not have explicit scale/precision defined in the source schema; assume standard Odoo currency precision (typically 2-4 decimal places).
