# odoo_account_payment

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the accounting and invoicing module. The naming conventions (e.g., `move_id`, `journal_id`, `partner_id`) and the presence of POS-related fields (`pos_session_id`, `pos_order_id`) are characteristic of the Odoo `account.payment` model.

## Functional process 
This table supports the cash management and accounts receivable/payable process. It tracks incoming and outgoing payments, their reconciliation status against accounting entries (`move_id`), and their association with business partners and financial journals. It acts as the central ledger for payment lifecycle management, from initiation to reconciliation.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single payment transaction record within the Odoo accounting system. It captures the financial details, status, and linkage to accounting moves and business partners. This is a raw landed copy of the Odoo `account_payment` table, serving as the base for downstream financial reporting and reconciliation analysis.

## Keys

- **Primary key (inferred):** `id`
- **Foreign keys (inferred):** 
    - `move_id` → `account_move.id` (Links payment to the general ledger entry)
    - `journal_id` → `account_journal.id` (Links to the financial journal)
    - `partner_id` → `res_partner.id` (Links to the business entity)
- **Natural keys (inferred):** 
    - `name` (Odoo typically issues a unique sequence number for payments)

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `customer_email` (if present in related tables) or `partner_id` links may require masking for GDPR/PII compliance.
- **Timestamps:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically does not use soft deletes; records are usually updated or cancelled (state change).
- **Precision:** `amount` and `amount_company_currency_signed` are `NUMERIC` types; ensure downstream systems handle arbitrary precision to avoid rounding errors.
- **State:** Always filter by `state` if you only want finalized/posted payments, as `draft` payments may not represent actual financial movement.
