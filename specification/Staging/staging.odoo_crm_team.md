# odoo_crm_team

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the CRM module. The naming convention (e.g., `crm_team`, `use_leads`, `assignment_domain`) and the presence of Odoo-specific audit columns like `create_uid` and `write_uid` are characteristic of the Odoo PostgreSQL schema.

## Functional process 
This table supports the Sales Team management process within the CRM. It defines the organizational structure for sales departments, determining how leads and opportunities are assigned, whether specific teams are enabled for lead/opportunity tracking, and setting financial targets for team performance.

## Description
One row represents a single sales team or department within the Odoo CRM configuration. This table serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `crm_team` model, capturing the configuration, ownership, and functional capabilities of each team. It is used in the staging layer to provide a foundation for downstream reporting on sales performance and team-based lead distribution.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `name` and `lead_properties_definition` may contain internal business terminology; no PII is expected, but review for internal-only naming conventions.
- **Timestamps:** All `create_date` and `write_date` values are stored in UTC.
- **Soft Deletes:** The `active` column acts as a soft-delete flag. Queries should generally filter by `WHERE active = TRUE` unless performing historical audits.
- **JSONB:** The `name` column is JSONB; ensure you use the `->>` operator to extract text values (e.g., `name->>'en_US'`).
- **Data Pattern:** This is a raw staging table; expect Odoo-specific internal IDs that may not map directly to external business entities without joining to `res_` tables.
