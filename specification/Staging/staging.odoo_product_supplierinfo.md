# odoo_product_supplierinfo

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the `product.supplierinfo` model. The naming convention (e.g., `product_tmpl_id`, `partner_id`, `write_uid`) and the presence of Odoo-standard audit fields (`create_date`, `write_date`) are characteristic of the Odoo PostgreSQL schema.

## Functional process 
This table supports the procurement and supply chain management process. It stores vendor-specific pricing, lead times, and product identifiers, allowing the system to determine which supplier to use for a given product, the expected cost, and the minimum quantity required for purchase orders.

## Description
One row in this table represents a specific supplier's price list entry or lead-time configuration for a product or product template. It serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `product_supplierinfo` table, capturing the relationship between vendors and internal product definitions within the staging layer.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** Contains vendor pricing and internal product codes; ensure access is restricted to procurement and finance analysts.
- **Timestamps:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC as per Odoo standard behavior.
- **Soft Deletes:** This table does not implement a soft-delete flag; records are typically removed or updated directly in the source.
- **Precision:** `min_qty`, `price`, and `discount` are `NUMERIC` types; ensure downstream systems handle decimal precision correctly to avoid rounding errors.
- **Nullability:** `product_id` and `product_tmpl_id` can both be null or populated depending on whether the supplier info is defined at the variant or template level.
