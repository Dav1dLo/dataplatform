# odoo_product_template_attribute_value

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the product configuration module. The naming convention `product_template_attribute_value` and the presence of `product_tmpl_id` and `attribute_id` are characteristic of Odoo's relational schema for managing product variants and their associated attributes (e.g., size, color).

## Functional process 
This table supports the product catalog and variant management process. It links specific attribute values (like "Red" or "Large") to product templates and attribute lines, allowing the system to calculate price adjustments (`price_extra`) for specific product configurations during the sales or e-commerce checkout process.

## Description
One row in this table represents a specific attribute value assigned to a product template, defining a configuration option for a product variant. It acts as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `product_template_attribute_value` model, capturing the relationship between product templates, attributes, and their associated pricing or visual properties.
