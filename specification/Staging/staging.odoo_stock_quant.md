# odoo_stock_quant

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the Inventory (Stock) module. The naming convention `stock_quant` and the presence of columns like `product_id`, `location_id`, and `lot_id` are characteristic of Odoo's internal inventory tracking schema.

## Functional process 
This table supports the inventory management and warehouse operations process. It tracks the current stock levels (quantities) of products across various locations, including reserved quantities for pending orders and inventory adjustment data.

## Description
One row in this table represents the current stock quantity of a specific product held in a particular location, potentially associated with a specific lot or package. It serves as a raw landed copy of Odoo's `stock.quant` model, providing the real-time snapshot of inventory levels required for stock valuation and availability reporting.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Timestamps:** All timestamps (`in_date`, `create_date`, `write_date`) are assumed to be in UTC, consistent with Odoo's internal storage format.
- **Soft Deletes:** This table represents current state; Odoo typically performs hard deletes on `stock_quant` records when stock reaches zero, though this may vary by version.
- **Precision:** `NUMERIC` types do not have explicit scale/precision defined in the source metadata; assume standard Odoo inventory precision (usually 2 or 3 decimal places).
- **Sensitivity:** No direct PII, though `user_id` links to internal staff identities.
