# odoo_rating_rating

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the `rating` module. The naming convention (e.g., `res_model`, `res_id`, `partner_id`) and the presence of Odoo-specific audit fields like `create_uid` and `write_uid` are characteristic of the Odoo framework's generic rating system used across various business objects.

## Functional process 
This table supports the customer feedback and satisfaction tracking process. It captures ratings associated with various Odoo business documents (such as tasks, helpdesk tickets, or project milestones), allowing the organization to measure service quality and collect qualitative feedback from partners or customers.

## Description
One row represents a single rating event or feedback submission linked to a specific Odoo record. This is a raw landed copy of the Odoo `rating.rating` model, serving as the staging layer entity for all feedback-related analytics.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **PII:** The `feedback` and `partner_id` columns may contain PII or sensitive customer feedback; ensure appropriate masking for non-authorized users.
- **Timezones:** Timestamps (`create_date`, `write_date`, `publisher_datetime`) are stored in UTC as per Odoo standard behavior.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically performs hard deletes on records unless a specific `active` field is present; this table does not contain an `active` column, implying hard deletes are likely.
- **Data Quality:** `res_model` and `res_id` are polymorphic; ensure queries filter by `res_model` to avoid joining against the wrong target tables.
