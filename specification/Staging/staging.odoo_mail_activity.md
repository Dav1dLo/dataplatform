# odoo_mail_activity

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, a modular business management software. The naming convention (`mail_activity`, `res_model`, `create_uid`) and the specific structure of linking activities to generic models via `res_model_id` and `res_id` are characteristic of the Odoo framework's messaging and activity tracking system.

## Functional process 
This table supports the CRM and task management processes within Odoo, specifically the "Activity" module. It tracks scheduled tasks, follow-ups, and communications (calls, meetings, emails) associated with various business entities like leads, opportunities, or sales orders, allowing users to manage their daily workflows and deadlines.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single scheduled activity or task assigned to a user within the Odoo platform. It captures the activity's status, deadline, and associated business record, serving as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `mail.activity` model for downstream staging and transformation.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `note` and `summary` columns may contain PII or sensitive business communication; ensure appropriate masking if exposing to non-authorized users.
- **Timezones:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC, but `user_tz` is provided as a string; ensure conversions are handled if local time reporting is required.
- **Soft Deletes:** The `active` column acts as a soft-delete flag. Queries should generally filter by `WHERE active = TRUE` unless performing historical audits.
- **Polymorphism:** The `res_model` and `res_id` columns represent a polymorphic relationship. Joining this table requires a `CASE` statement or multiple joins to resolve the target record name based on the `res_model` value.
