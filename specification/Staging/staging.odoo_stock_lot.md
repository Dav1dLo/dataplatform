# odoo_stock_lot

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the Inventory/Warehouse management module. The naming convention (`stock_lot`, `product_id`, `company_id`) and the presence of Odoo-specific audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, `write_date`) are characteristic of the Odoo PostgreSQL schema.

## Functional process 
This table supports the inventory tracking and traceability process. It manages "Lots" or "Serial Numbers" assigned to products, allowing the business to track specific batches of inventory through the supply chain, from receipt to final delivery or consumption.

## Description
One row in this table represents a unique lot or serial number assigned to a specific product within the Odoo inventory system. This is a raw landed staging table containing the current state of lot records, serving as the foundation for downstream inventory valuation and traceability reporting.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** The `lot_properties` and `note` fields may contain internal business comments or sensitive batch information; review for PII if applicable.
- **Timestamps:** All `create_date` and `write_date` values are stored in UTC as per standard Odoo behavior.
- **Data Integrity:** This is a raw staging table; it may contain duplicates or incomplete records if the Odoo extraction process encountered partial syncs.
- **JSONB Usage:** `lot_properties` and `standard_price` require PostgreSQL JSONB operators (e.g., `->>`) for extraction; ensure downstream pipelines handle schema evolution within these fields.
