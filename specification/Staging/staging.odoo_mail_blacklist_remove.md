# odoo_mail_blacklist_remove

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the `mail` module. The naming convention `mail_blacklist_remove` and the presence of standard Odoo audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`, `create_date`, `write_date`) are characteristic of Odoo's internal ORM structure.

## Functional process 
This table supports the email marketing and communication management process by tracking requests to remove email addresses from a blacklist. It serves as an audit log or processing queue for users who have opted back into receiving communications after previously being blacklisted.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single request to remove an email address from the system's communication blacklist. It acts as a raw landing copy of the Odoo `mail.blacklist.remove` model, capturing the email, the reason for removal, and the audit trail of who processed the request and when.
