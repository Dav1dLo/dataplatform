# odoo_account_payment_method_line

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the accounting module. The naming convention `account_payment_method_line` and the presence of `journal_id` and `payment_method_id` are characteristic of Odoo's internal schema for managing payment method configurations within accounting journals.

## Functional process 
This table supports the configuration of payment methods for accounting journals. It defines which payment methods are available for specific journals and links them to corresponding accounting accounts, facilitating the automated reconciliation and recording of payments in the general ledger.

## Description
One row in this table represents a specific payment method configuration line associated with an accounting journal. It acts as a raw landed copy of the Odoo configuration entity, capturing the relationship between payment methods, journals, and their associated ledger accounts.
