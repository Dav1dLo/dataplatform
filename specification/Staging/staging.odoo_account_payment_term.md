# odoo_account_payment_term

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP. The naming convention `account_payment_term` and the presence of Odoo-specific columns like `create_uid`, `write_uid`, and `JSONB` fields for multi-language support are characteristic of the Odoo accounting module's metadata structures.

## Functional process 
This table supports the accounts receivable and payable business processes by defining payment terms (e.g., "Net 30", "2% 10, Net 30"). It governs how invoices are aged and whether early payment discounts are applied, directly influencing cash flow management and invoice generation logic.

## Description
One row represents a single payment term configuration available within the Odoo accounting system. It acts as a raw landed copy from the Odoo staging layer, capturing the rules for payment deadlines and associated discount calculations. The grain is one row per unique payment term definition.
