# odoo_account_analytic_distribution_model

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP. The naming convention `account_analytic_distribution_model` and the presence of columns like `partner_id`, `product_id`, and `analytic_distribution` are characteristic of Odoo's accounting and analytic accounting modules.

## Functional process 
This table supports the automated analytic accounting distribution process. It defines rules that automatically assign analytic accounts to journal items based on specific criteria such as the business partner, product, product category, or account prefix, facilitating granular cost and revenue tracking across the organization.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single configuration rule for analytic distribution. It acts as a raw landed copy of the Odoo model definition, capturing the conditions (e.g., specific partner or product) and the resulting distribution mapping stored in JSONB format.
