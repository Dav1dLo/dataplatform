# odoo_mrp_production

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the Manufacturing (MRP) module. The naming convention (`mrp_production`, `picking_type_id`, `bom_id`) and the presence of Odoo-specific audit columns (`create_uid`, `write_uid`) are characteristic of the Odoo PostgreSQL schema.

## Functional process 
This table supports the manufacturing execution process, tracking the lifecycle of production orders from planning to completion. It manages the consumption of raw materials, the production of finished goods, and the routing of inventory between locations, integrating with procurement groups and sales orders to fulfill demand.

## Description
One row represents a single manufacturing order (MO) within the Odoo system. It captures the production requirements, including the product to be manufactured, the Bill of Materials (BoM) used, the planned quantities, and the current state of the production workflow. This table serves as the raw landed staging entity for all production-related activities.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Sensitive Data:** No direct PII, but `name` and `origin` may contain internal business references.
- **Timezones:** Timestamps (`create_date`, `date_start`, etc.) are stored in UTC by Odoo.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically does not use soft deletes; records are usually permanent unless purged.
- **Precision:** `product_qty` and `qty_producing` are `NUMERIC`; ensure downstream systems handle decimal precision correctly to avoid rounding errors in inventory counts.
