# odoo_sms_template

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the SMS Marketing or base notification module. The naming convention (`odoo_sms_template`), the presence of `model_id` and `model` (standard Odoo fields for linking templates to specific business objects), and the use of `JSONB` for multi-language content are characteristic of Odoo's PostgreSQL-based architecture.

## Functional process 
This table supports the automated communication and notification engine within Odoo. It stores reusable SMS templates that are dynamically populated with data from business objects (e.g., sales orders, appointments, or marketing campaigns) defined by the `model` field, facilitating personalized customer outreach.

## Description
One row represents a single SMS template definition, including its content, associated business model, and metadata. This is a raw landed copy of the Odoo `sms.template` model, serving as the staging layer source for downstream communication analytics and template management reporting.
