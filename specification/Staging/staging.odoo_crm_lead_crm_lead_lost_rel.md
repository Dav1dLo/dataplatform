# odoo_crm_lead_crm_lead_lost_rel

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the CRM module. The naming convention `_rel` and the presence of two foreign key columns indicate this is a junction table generated by Odoo's ORM to manage a many-to-many relationship between CRM leads and lost reason records.

## Functional process 
This table supports the sales pipeline management process, specifically tracking the association between lost sales opportunities and the reasons for their loss. It enables reporting on churn signals and lost-deal analysis by linking specific lead records to their corresponding loss metadata.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single association between a CRM lead and a specific "lost reason" entry. It serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo relational mapping table, maintaining the link between the `crm.lead` and `crm.lead.lost` models.
