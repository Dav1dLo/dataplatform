# odoo_mrp_bom

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, specifically the Manufacturing (MRP) module. The naming convention `mrp_bom` and the presence of columns like `product_tmpl_id`, `product_uom_id`, and `picking_type_id` are characteristic of Odoo's internal database schema for Bill of Materials management.

## Functional process 
This table supports the production planning and manufacturing process by defining the Bill of Materials (BoM). It dictates the structure of products, specifying the required components, quantities, and production parameters (such as `produce_delay` and `days_to_prepare_mo`) necessary to manufacture a finished good or sub-assembly.

## Description
One row in this table represents a single Bill of Materials definition, which outlines the recipe or component list for a specific product template. It serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo `mrp.bom` model, capturing the configuration for manufacturing operations at the grain of one BoM per product or product variant.

## Keys

- **Primary key (inferred):** `id`
- **Foreign keys (inferred):** 
    - `product_tmpl_id` → `product_template.id` (Standard Odoo link to product definitions).
    - `product_id` → `product_product.id` (Links to specific product variants).
    - `product_uom_id` → `uom_uom.id` (Links to unit of measure definitions).
    - `picking_type_id` → `stock_picking_type.id` (Links to warehouse operation types).
    - `company_id` → `res_company.id` (Links to organizational structure).
- **Natural keys (inferred):** 
    - `code` (Often used as a unique business reference for the BoM).

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Soft Deletes:** The `active` column acts as a soft-delete flag; ensure queries filter by `active = true` unless historical/archived data is explicitly required.
- **Timestamps:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC as per standard Odoo behavior.
- **Data Sensitivity:** No direct PII is present, but `create_uid` and `write_uid` link to internal user records which may contain sensitive employee information.
- **Precision:** `product_qty` is `NUMERIC` to support fractional quantities; ensure downstream systems handle decimal precision correctly.
