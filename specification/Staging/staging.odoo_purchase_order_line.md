# odoo_purchase_order_line

## Source system
This table originates from Odoo ERP, an open-source business management suite. The naming convention (e.g., `order_id`, `product_id`, `partner_id`, `create_uid`) and the presence of Odoo-specific fields like `analytic_distribution` and `qty_received_method` are characteristic of the Odoo `purchase.order.line` model.

## Functional process 
This table supports the procurement and "Procure-to-Pay" (P2P) business process. It tracks individual line items within purchase orders, managing the lifecycle of requested goods or services from initial planning (`date_planned`) and ordering (`product_qty`) through to receipt (`qty_received`) and invoicing (`qty_invoiced`).

## Description
One row represents a single line item within a purchase order, detailing the specific product, quantity, and pricing agreed upon with a vendor. This table serves as a raw landed copy of the Odoo purchase order line records, providing the granular data necessary for calculating procurement spend, vendor performance, and inventory replenishment requirements.

## Keys

- **Primary key (inferred):** `id`
- **Foreign keys (inferred):** 
    - `order_id` → `purchase_order.id` (Standard Odoo parent-child relationship).
    - `product_id` → `product_product.id` (Links to the product catalog).
    - `partner_id` → `res_partner.id` (Links to the vendor/supplier).
- **Natural keys (inferred):** 
    - None. Odoo relies on internal surrogate IDs for relational integrity.

## Caveats for downstream consumers

- **Timestamps:** `create_date` and `write_date` are stored in UTC.
- **Soft Deletes:** Odoo typically performs hard deletes on records; however, check for `active` flags if present in other related tables.
- **Precision:** `NUMERIC` fields are used for financial accuracy; ensure downstream systems maintain this scale to avoid rounding errors.
- **JSONB:** The `analytic_distribution` column requires PostgreSQL-specific JSONB operators for extraction.
- **Data Integrity:** `qty_received` and `qty_invoiced` may be updated asynchronously by warehouse or accounting modules; ensure joins account for potential lag.
